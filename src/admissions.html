📄 Admission Requirements

🎓 BS Programs (BSCS / BSSE)

Minimum 50% marks in FSc Pre-Engineering or equivalent (A-Levels, ICS with Mathematics). Entry test conducted by COMSATS. Valid CNIC or Form-B required. Age limit: 25 years.

🎓 MS Program (MSCS)

Minimum 16 years of education in a relevant field with CGPA 2.0 or 45% marks. GAT General (NTS) score of at least 50 required. BS/BE in CS, SE, IT, or equivalent disciplines.
