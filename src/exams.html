📝 Exam Rules & Guidelines

🔒 Entry Requirements

Students must bring their university ID card and admit card to the examination hall. Entry will be denied without valid identification. Report 15 minutes before the exam starts.

🚫 Prohibited Items

Mobile phones, smartwatches, and electronic devices are strictly prohibited. Students found with unauthorized material will face disciplinary action under the university plagiarism policy.

📋 Allowed Materials

Only university-issued answer sheets and question papers are allowed. Calculators are permitted for Mathematics and Engineering subjects only with prior approval from the department.


🏠 Examination Venues

Block A — Main Building

Rooms 101, 102, 103, 105. Capacity: 200 students per session. Air-conditioned. Entry from Main Gate.

Block B — Academic Block

Rooms 205, 210. Capacity: 120 students per session. Entry from East Gate near the cafeteria.

Block C & CS Labs

Room 301 and Lab 3. Capacity: 80 students. Used for practical and open-book exams with computer access.
